Area O1: where extraordinary aliens get their paperwork done.

O-1 gets you a visit. EB-1 lets you keep the spaceship.
Never bills you a single six minutes.

Your evidence file, built on your own computer. Area O1 turns your work (papers, code, talks, press, judging) into a living evidence file for an O-1A or EB-1A extraordinary-ability case: a criteria scoreboard, an evidence inbox, metrics, deadlines, letters and a dashboard. Start from your LinkedIn PDF; it takes a few minutes.

macOS / Linux: paste into Terminal

curl -LsSf https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ris3abh/areao1/main/install.sh | sh

Windows: paste into PowerShell

powershell -ExecutionPolicy ByPass -c "irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ris3abh/areao1/main/install.ps1 | iex"

It installs uv if you don't have it, installs Area O1, and opens it in your browser. Nothing else on your machine changes. The scripts are short; read install.sh or install.ps1 first. Afterwards, start it again with areao1.

What you need

A PDF, and that's it

  • Your LinkedIn profile as a PDF (on LinkedIn: Profile > More > Save to PDF). Area O1 reads it on your computer and removes emails and phone numbers first. You can also skip it.
  • Optionally, an OpenAI API key. Only chat and agent runs need it; everything else works without a model. Already use Claude Code or Codex? They can read your case over MCP, no key needed.
  • macOS, Linux or Windows, and an internet connection to install.

Privacy

It runs on your computer

  • The dashboard is served only to your own computer (127.0.0.1). There's no Area O1 account or server.
  • Your case is a private workspace folder (~/AreaO1): plain files in their own git repo. It's yours to keep, back up to a private remote, move or delete.
  • Area O1 goes online for what you ask (a lookup you said yes to, a source you import, the AI you connect, Gmail if you connect it) and to keep official rules current: government pages, the Federal Register and eCFR, and a public library of government-page snapshots.

What it does

Your case, one place, always current

  • An evidence inbox and a scoreboard. Your papers, code, talks, press and judging become proposals you accept; each criterion shows banked, building or a gap. An invitation is never counted as done.
  • Memory as a sky. Every fact is a star that quotes its source; click one to trace it back, or replay how your case grew.
  • Verified opportunities. Invitations in your mail are checked against the sender's authentication and the event's official page before they're called verified.
  • Rules that stay current. Answers are checked against official sources that refresh when a rule actually changes.
  • Letters from facts. Drafts built only from what you approved, for your writers to rewrite and sign. Nothing is sent without your approval.
