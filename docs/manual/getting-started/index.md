# Getting started

Install Area O1, walk through onboarding, and decide whether you want the AI features.

Area O1 runs on your own computer and keeps your case in a private folder of plain files. Most people are set up
in a few minutes.

| [Costs and budget caps](costs.md) | What the AI features cost, the per-run and monthly caps, and what's free |

!!! note "Not legal advice"
    Area O1 organizes your evidence. It doesn't tell you whether you qualify. Confirm your strategy with an
    immigration attorney.
