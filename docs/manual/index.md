# Area O1 docs

Area O1 turns your work (papers, code, talks, press, judging) into a living evidence file for an O-1A or
EB-1A extraordinary-ability case: a criteria scoreboard, an evidence inbox, metrics, deadlines, letters and a
dashboard. It runs on your computer, and your case is a folder of plain files that stays yours.

## In one minute

1. **Install** with one line, and Area O1 opens in your browser at `127.0.0.1:7777`.
2. **Start from your LinkedIn PDF.** It's read on your computer, with emails and phone numbers removed first.
3. **Add sources**: your GitHub, Hugging Face, scholarly profiles, press pages. Findings land in the
   **Inbox**, each quoting its source word for word.
4. **Review.** Nothing becomes evidence until you accept it, and an invitation never counts as a completion.
5. **Watch the scoreboard**: each criterion is banked, building or a gap, against the profile's rules.

!!! note "Not legal advice"
    Area O1 is not legal advice and is not affiliated with USCIS. Criteria profiles are community-maintained
    summaries of public regulations (8 CFR 214.2(o), 8 CFR 204.5(h)). Always confirm strategy with an
    immigration attorney. [Read the disclaimer](reference/disclaimer.md).
