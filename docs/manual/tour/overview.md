# Overview

The page you land on: where your case stands against the threshold, and what needs you this week.

![The Overview page: 2 of 3 criteria banked, one candidate in the Inbox, the scoreboard and next deadlines](../assets/shots/overview-dark.webp#only-dark)


## What's on it

- **Criteria banked / needed.** The big number is how many criteria are *banked* against the profile's
  threshold (3 for O-1A and EB-1A), with the target you set (5 by default). The bar shows each criterion:
  filled when banked, partly filled when building.
- **Inbox.** How many candidates wait for your review. Click through to decide on them.
- **Filing target.** Your target filing date, once you add a filing deadline on the [Calendar](calendar.md).
- **This week.** An agent-written briefing of what changed and three things to do, with inline approvals.
  Press **Refresh** for one now, or turn on the daily *What changed* mission in
  [Settings](settings.md). Next to it, **Only you can do these** lists the things no agent should do for
  you, like reviewing Inbox items or asking a writer for a letter.
- **Criteria scoreboard.** Every criterion with its status (banked, building, gap or dropped), its number of
  counted exhibits, and what it still needs ("needs 1 more exhibit", "2 signals"). Click one to open it on
  [Evidence](evidence.md).
- **Next deadlines** and **Metrics**: the closest deadlines and the latest numbers from your sources.

## How the scoreboard decides

A criterion is **banked** when it has enough counted exhibits *and* enough distinct strength signals for the
profile's rule, **building** when it has at least one counted exhibit (or evidence still at an earlier stage),
and a **gap** otherwise. Only completed evidence counts: an invitation to judge is not judging. See
[Invited vs completed](../how-it-works/invited-vs-completed.md).

!!! note
    The scoreboard is a rule-based checklist against community-maintained summaries of the regulations. It
    doesn't say whether you qualify. Confirm strategy with an immigration attorney.
