# Contacts and Mail

The people your case depends on (letter writers, organizers, editors, collaborators) and the mail that
matters to it.

## People

![The Contacts page with a follow-up email to Omar Haddad waiting for approval](../assets/shots/contacts-light.webp#only-light)
![The Contacts page with a follow-up email to Omar Haddad waiting for approval](../assets/shots/contacts-dark.webp#only-dark)


- **Contacts** have a name, emails, a relationship and an organization. They're for tracking only and never
  count as evidence.
- With Gmail connected, **Refresh threads** finds your threads with each contact and shows the last touch and
  when to follow up.
- **Emails waiting for your approval** holds every draft Area O1 wrote: follow-ups after quiet days, check-ins
  to organizers, letter drafts for writers. Edit the subject and text, then **Approve & send**, or **Reject**.
  After you approve there are 10 seconds to **Undo send**.
- The header shows how many emails went out today against your daily limit, and failed sends are listed so
  you can retry.

## Mail

![The Mail view: case mail sorted into categories, with a verified sender badge on a judging invitation](../assets/shots/mail-light.webp#only-light)
![The Mail view: case mail sorted into categories, with a verified sender badge on a judging invitation](../assets/shots/mail-dark.webp#only-dark)


**Mail** shows case-relevant mail only, read-only:

- Categories: **Invites**, **Judging & hackathons**, **Reviewer requests**, **Letter writers**,
  **Press & media**, **Awards & memberships** and **Other contact threads**. Filter by contact with
  *Showing*.
- Mail is sorted by rules first. Sorting with the model is an opt-in setting (Settings > Gmail).
- **Move to…** files a message under another category, and the rules learn from it.
- Each message shows its sender check (**verified sender**, failed or unverified) and, when it became an
  Inbox candidate, a link to it.
- **Bring in emails from another account (e.g. work)** explains how to bring mail in without connecting that
  account: save it as `.eml` and drop it here, or forward it as an attachment.

Nothing is marked read, moved, labelled or deleted in Gmail. Only who, when and the subject are kept; a
message's text is fetched when you open it and never saved.
