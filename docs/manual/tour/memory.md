# Memory

Everything Area O1 knows about your case, drawn as a night sky: one cluster per criterion, one star per claim.

## Reading the sky

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| A bright, large star | High confidence |
| A solid star | Approved by you |
| A ring | Waiting for your review |
| A red glow | In conflict with another claim |
| A faded star | Superseded by a newer value |

Inside a cluster the oldest claims sit at the center and newer ones spiral outward. The sky is dark in both
themes.

## Exploring

- **Filters**: criterion, entity (a paper, repo, event or person), a date, and status (approved, pending,
  superseded, rejected).
- **Click a star** to open its trail: the claim, the verified excerpt, the raw source it came from, the
  reviews, earlier versions, and the exhibits that cite it. On the sky, the trail draws a path to the source
  and to any exhibit.
- **The time slider and Replay** show the case up to a date, or grow it in date order.
- **Zoom** with the buttons, the mouse wheel or a pinch; drag to pan; **Fit the whole sky** resets.
- **List** shows the same claims as a list, for screen readers and keyboards.

With reduced motion turned on in your system settings, stars don't fade in or twinkle and Replay jumps to the
end.
