# Pipeline

In-flight work that could become evidence: ideas, applications, and things you're waiting to hear back on.

![The Pipeline board with Idea, Applied, Waiting and Done columns](../assets/shots/pipeline-light.webp#only-light)
![The Pipeline board with Idea, Applied, Waiting and Done columns](../assets/shots/pipeline-dark.webp#only-dark)


## How it works

- Four columns: **Idea**, **Applied**, **Waiting** and **Done**. Add an idea at the top of the first column,
  then drag cards between columns, or use the arrow buttons on a card.
- From the keyboard: Tab to a card and press the left or right arrow key to move it one column. The focus moves
  with the card, so you can keep going.
- A card can name the criterion it would help, a link, and a follow-up date. Follow-ups show on the
  [Calendar](calendar.md) and in the calendar feed.
- A card with no movement for 14 days or more is flagged **stale**, and the weekly digest mentions it.

Moving a card to **Done** doesn't create evidence. When the thing happened, upload the proof on
[Evidence](evidence.md) or accept it from the [Inbox](inbox.md). A done card with a criterion gets a proof
checklist (see [Proof recipes](../how-it-works/proof-recipes.md)), and the card shows how many items are still
to save.

The board is `data/pipeline.json` in your workspace.
